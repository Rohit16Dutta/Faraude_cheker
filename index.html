How to stay safe

  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords — not with your bank, not with "support", not with anyone.
  • Go direct. Instead of clicking a link, type the company's address yourself or use their official app.
  • Slow down. Urgency and threats are designed to make you act before you think.
  • Call back on a number you trust — from the back of your card or the official website, not the message.
  • No one legitimate asks for gift cards or crypto as payment.

Already been scammed?

Contact your bank immediately to freeze payments, change any passwords you shared, and report it:

  • 🇮🇳 India: call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in
  • 🇺🇸 USA: reportfraud.ftc.gov
  • 🇬🇧 UK: forward scam texts to 7726, report at actionfraud.police.uk
